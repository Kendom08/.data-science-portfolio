## Project Showcase

### Diamond Pricing Analysis
- **Research Question**: What drives diamond prices — carat, cut, or color?
- **Status**: Completed

![Carat vs Price](https://github.com/Kendom08/.data-science-project/blob/main/carat_vs_price.png?raw=true)

Carat weight shows a clear positive relationship with price — larger diamonds cost significantly more.

![Price by Cut](https://github.com/Kendom08/.data-science-project/blob/main/price_by_cut.png?raw=true)

Counterintuitively, Ideal-cut diamonds have a lower median price than Fair-cut ones. This is because buyers choose premium cuts for smaller stones, while large rare diamonds may have lower cut grades.

![Count by Color](https://github.com/Kendom08/.data-science-project/blob/main/count_by_color.png?raw=true)

Color grade E and G diamonds are the most common in this dataset.

![Price by Color](https://github.com/Kendom08/.data-science-project/blob/main/price_by_color.png?raw=true)

Better color grades (D, E) do not show higher median prices, as the effect is confounded by carat size.
